# areas/ — ongoing responsibilities

Standards you maintain over time with **no end date**: marketing, SEO,
sales, finance, operations, hiring. Unlike projects, an area is never
"done" — you keep a level of quality in it.

Use areas for durable knowledge a responsibility accumulates: your SEO
playbook, the sales pipeline notes, recurring-content guidelines. Recurring
deliverables live here too — e.g. `marketing/` holds content, SEO files,
and campaign exports as they're produced.

```
areas/
├── marketing/
├── seo/
├── sales/
└── finance/
```

One-off generated documents that don't belong to any area go to
`resources/documents/` instead. If an area stops being maintained, move it
to `archive/`.
